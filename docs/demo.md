# Demo guide

## Purpose and preparation
Demonstrate a product workflow and the user's ability to reason about AI and testing. The owner has a live workspace explicitly used for testing and accepts sample data being added there. Verify the selected workspace and avoid any real customer material. AI analysis can incur cost and may give different classifications from run to run.

## Suggested walkthrough
1. Introduce the problem: feedback is scattered, and product teams need traceable evidence.
2. Upload fictional survey, transcript or email data in the selected test workspace; explain what extraction and provenance are.
3. Open Register, run AI analysis, review suggested type/sentiment/theme, then correct or approve a specific item.
4. Show the theme evidence and trend, with a clear note that the indicator depends on approved feedback dates.
5. Show roadmap promotion and digest if configured; explain which data a share link reveals. Do not generate or rotate live integration credentials for a demo without consent.
6. Show local Playwright results or the CI run; explain which cases are skipped and that a green test is not complete proof.

The AI may assign themes differently and similar sentences may not be duplicates. Rehearse with the actual data; don't promise a low-confidence result or a particular count. If speaking in an interview, accurately describe the AI assistant's contribution and your own role.
